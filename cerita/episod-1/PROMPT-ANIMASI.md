Create one wide 16:9 image containing exactly FOUR equal wide 16:9 panels in a precise 2 by 2 grid, no gaps, labels or borders. Image references in order correspond to top-left classroom, top-right food preparation room, bottom-left rainy carnival canopy, bottom-right sunset packing booth. Recreate each corresponding complete reference environment and the exact foreground desk/table, food ingredients, bowls, lemon drink jug, sealed sandwich containers and cardboard packing box. Preserve reference camera perspective, warm anime rendering, morning/golden/rain/sunset lighting and scene layout. NO people, faces, arms, hands, silhouettes or mannequins anywhere. Keep ingredients and table foreground rich and detailed. Empty middle foreground where separately animated seated/standing characters will be composited.
